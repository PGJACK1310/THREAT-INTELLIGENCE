# Threat Intelligence Life Cycle

## 📘 Definition

> Threat Intelligence is evidence-based knowledge — including context, mechanisms, indicators, implications, and actionable advice — about an existing or emerging menace or hazard to assets.
>
> The Threat Intelligence Life Cycle is a structured, continuous process that organizations use to plan, collect, process, analyze, and disseminate intelligence so that decision-makers can act with speed, accuracy, and confidence.
>
> Unlike ad-hoc vulnerability scanning, a mature TI programme turns raw data into contextually rich, operationally relevant insights that drive proactive defense.
